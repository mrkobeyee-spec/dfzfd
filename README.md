To play flappy bird, tap the screen, click your mouse, or press the space bar to make the bird flap it's wings and rise upwards. 
Between taps, a constant downward pull of gravity forces the bird to drop. Your goal is to time your flaps carefully to guide the bird through the gaps of 
incoming green pipes without hitting the structures or crashing into the ground. Each pair of pipes you successfully pass awards you one point, and the game ends
immediately if you collide with any obstacle.
